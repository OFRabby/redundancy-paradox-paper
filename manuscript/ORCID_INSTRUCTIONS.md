# ORCID Registration (5-minute task)

## Steps

1. Go to https://orcid.org/register
2. Use institutional or personal email
3. Verify email
4. Set visibility to "Everyone"
5. Copy your ORCID iD (format: 0000-0000-0000-0000)

## After registration, update:

- `MLWA_COMPLIANCE.md` — author ORCID field
- `cover_letter.md` — author line
- `manuscript.tex` — author affiliation (add ORCID)
- Title page (if required by journal)
